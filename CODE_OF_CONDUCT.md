# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback; and
- accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
sexualized language or imagery, publishing private information, trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments, and other conduct that would be considered
unprofessional in a community setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct
and may take appropriate corrective action in response to behavior they consider
unacceptable or threatening.

## Scope and reporting

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project. Report unacceptable behavior privately
through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Idriz-hax/harbor-ledger-memory/security/advisories/new).
This is also the confidential channel for conduct reports when a public issue
or pull request would be unsafe. Public issues and pull requests are not
required for conduct reports.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter when handling
a report.
